Our Services

Expert Waterproofing Services For Every Need

We provide advanced waterproofing solutions for residential, commercial and industrial spaces using high-quality materials and proven technique — matched to the surface and the actual cause, not a one-size-fits-all coating.

Roof & Terrace Waterproofing

Concrete and metal roofs, terraces and roof gardens sealed against monsoon leaks with membrane and PU coating systems.

  • Concrete roof & terrace waterproofing
  • Metal roof & gutter waterproofing
  • Roof garden waterproofing
  • Heat-reducing roof coating

Exterior Wall Waterproofing

Fortifying external walls and facades against wind-driven rain and seepage before it reaches your interiors.

  • Exterior wall waterproofing coatings
  • Dampness & paint waterproofing
  • Rising dampness treatment

Water Tank Waterproofing

Overhead and underground water tanks lined and sealed to keep stored water clean and tanks leak-free.

  • Underground water tank waterproofing
  • Overhead concrete tank waterproofing
  • Anti-bacterial tank coating

Bathroom & Toilet Waterproofing

The most common leak source in Indian homes, treated at the source rather than painted over.

  • Bathroom & toilet waterproofing
  • Wet-area & sanitary waterproofing
  • Tile joint epoxy filling

Basement Waterproofing

Interior and exterior basement treatment to stop seepage through walls, floors and construction joints.

  • Basement wall & floor waterproofing
  • Structural crack sealing
  • Works alongside our drainage system below

Interior Basement Drainage System

A perimeter drain channel that collects and redirects groundwater away from the foundation when sealing alone isn't enough.

  • Interior perimeter drain installation
  • Sump pit & pump integration
  • Pairs well with basement waterproofing

Crack Repair & Structural Repairs

Cracks are a symptom, not the problem — we identify the cause and repair it so it doesn't return.

  • Structural crack repair
  • Building rehabilitation
  • Concrete spalling repair

Expansion Joint Treatment

Movement joints sealed with the right flexible system so they keep working through every season.

  • Expansion joint repair
  • Construction joint sealing

PU Injection Grouting

Polyurethane resin injected under pressure to seal active leaks and moving cracks from the inside out.

  • Active leak sealing via PU injection
  • Moving-crack & joint injection
  • Works in wet, live-leak conditions

Cementitious Injection Grouting

Cement-based grout pumped into voids and cracks for structural, non-moving joints and water-stop applications.

  • Structural void & crack filling
  • Water-stop grouting
  • Foundation & retaining-wall grouting

Bituminous Waterproofing

Bitumen-based membranes and coatings — a proven, economical system for roofs, foundations and below-grade surfaces.

  • Bituminous membrane application
  • Bitumen coating for foundations
  • Below-grade waterproofing

Industrial Waterproofing

Large-scale waterproofing for factories, warehouses and plant structures, built to handle industrial wear and chemical exposure.

  • Factory & warehouse roof waterproofing
  • Chemical-resistant coatings
  • Large-format industrial floor & tank work

Swimming Pool Waterproofing

Pool shells and decks waterproofed to hold water safely and resist chemical exposure.

  • Swimming pool shell waterproofing
  • Pool deck waterproofing

Pipe & Drain Waterproofing

Pipe penetrations and drains sealed at the collar, the point where most slab leaks start.

  • Pipe collar waterproofing
  • Drain & sump waterproofing

How We Work

A Straightforward Five-Step Process

01

Site inspection

We inspect the affected area, check for the true source of the leak, and photograph the damage.

02

Written estimate

You get a clear, itemised quote — materials, labour and timeline — before any work begins.

03

Surface preparation

Loose plaster, old coatings and debris are removed so the new system bonds properly.

04

Application & curing

Membrane, coating, grouting or injection system applied by trained crew and left to cure fully.

05

Final check & warranty
