// Why It Fails Elsewhere

Most leaks aren't fixed. They're postponed.

A coat of sealant on a visible crack looks like a fix, but it doesn't stop water moving through capillary action or building up pressure behind a wall. That's why the same patch job gets redone every year.

Blue Paradise Enterprises, under the direction of incharge Matthew Adgale, inspects for the actual source before recommending a system — so the job holds through the next monsoon, not just the next inspection.
